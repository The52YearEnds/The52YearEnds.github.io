Welcome to the show

Pull up a chair. The tea's still warm, and I'm glad you found the place.

I'm a lifelong nerd who runs D&D twice a week, which mostly means building kingdoms so my players can find new ways to set them on fire. By day, I work in finance somewhere cold in the Midwest. By night, I'm earning a dual master's, an MBA and an MS in Management Information Systems, because one degree felt too sensible.

This is where the stories go. Every Friday for a year, something new goes up: the next chapter, or a standalone that wandered in from one of my worlds.
